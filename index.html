Methodology

What counts as market capitalization

Public companies

Equity market capitalization is calculated as share price multiplied by shares outstanding. On Vercel, API-backed rows can refresh hourly through the registry endpoint.

Commodities

Gold and silver are estimated from above-ground supply multiplied by spot price. These are methodology estimates, not exchange-listed market caps.

Cryptoassets

Crypto market capitalization is circulating supply multiplied by the latest traded market price.

Private companies

Private valuations use the latest credible disclosed funding round, fund mark, tender offer, IPO target, or transaction report, and are labeled separately from live public market caps.
